# Content audit: portfolio v3 to multi-page site

Source: `docs/reference/portfolio-v3-forest.html`. Rewritten copy: `src/data/copy.json`.

## What is strong

- **The hero line.** "Content that makes your brand look *as good as it is*" is clear, warm and about the client. Kept.
- **The scope statement.** "I don't promise follower, lead or sales numbers" builds trust. Kept, and it now also says that regulated content gets checked by a qualified person on the client's side.
- **Case-study depth.** Brand kit, voice, pillars, grid logic, caption, alt text and four reasoned creatives per brand. The best selling point.
- **Honest labelling.** Sample reviews, [Name] placeholders and the footer disclaimer.

## What is weak, repetitive or unclear

- **Repetition.** "On-brand, on time" and "design and copy in one place" recur in four sections. Now used once or twice across pages.
- **"6 brand case studies"** in the hero facts could read as six real clients. Changed to "sample brands, built end to end".
- **"Most popular" badge** on the Content package implies sales data that does not exist. Changed to "A good place to start".
- **Contact promise over-reaches.** "Ideas and a fixed monthly quote within one working day" comes before any call. Now: reply within one working day, quote within two working days of the call.
- **"First post in two weeks"** depends on the client. Now "in about two weeks", with the dependency explained in the FAQ.
- **"Website content upkeep"** was undefined. A one-line detail now says what it covers.
- **Channel mismatch.** Lowtide lists TikTok but has no TikTok deliverable. Halden is "Leicester" in the logo but "[Town]" in posts. Flagged for the developer: the case copy keeps the reference channels, and Halden's posts should show Leicester or keep the placeholder consistently.
- **No narrative.** Each case now has a brief, challenge, approach and deliverables.
