Why the work looks the way it does

Every creative above is built on a format that's working right now. These are the trends I design for, drawn from recent platform research.

Product brands · Instagram & Facebook

Reels · 3-second hook

Reels lead, and they work on mute

Reels now take about half of all time spent on Instagram. The best hook viewers in three seconds and carry on-screen text for sound-off viewing.

Source 1
Repeatable series

A weekly series beats one-offs

A recurring format with one fixed template builds recognition and loyalty faster than unrelated posts.

Source 1
Raw & relatable · Customer content

Real beats perfect

Honest, less-produced posts and reposted customer content are outperforming studio-perfect feeds.

Sources 1, 2
Photo dump · Save-worthy carousel

Photo dumps and teaching carousels

Five to ten slides mixing polished and candid shots feel personal; carousels that teach something earn saves.

Source 2
Editorial minimalism · Collab post

Grain, space and handwriting

Film grain, one subject with room to breathe, and handwritten type all signal a human touch. Collab posts share one post across two grids.

Sources 2, 4
Grid & search

The 3:4 grid and Instagram SEO

Profile grids preview at 3:4, so key content sits in the centre. Keyword captions and alt text help posts get found.

Sources 3, 1

Service brands · LinkedIn & Facebook

PDF carousel · 4:5

PDF carousels, done properly

Portrait slides, 5 to 15 of them, one idea and under 60 words each, with 100 to 200 words of post copy.

Source 5
People over logos

People reach further than logos

Carousels from personal profiles see around 63% more engagement than company-page ones, so I write content for your team to post as themselves.

Source 5
Raw screenshots · Lo-fi expert video

Proof over polish

First-person detail, real screenshots and simple subtitled expert videos outperform generic templates.

Source 6
Zero-click value

The whole answer, in the post

Posts that deliver their full value in the feed are favoured over posts that push a link.

Source 6
Newsletter with a fixed shape

Repeatable newsletters

A fixed structure, such as 3 proof points, 2 takes and 1 risk, makes every issue quick to read and quick to write.

Source 6
Moment-led post

Timed to real moments

Posts tied to a moment people already care about, like the first cold snap or a tax deadline, beat generic offers.
